This app requires a BTicino system using the Door Entry mobile app (such as EasyKit). 

Seamlessly control your gates and intercoms through Homey. Automatically unlock gates when you arrive home, receive instant notifications when someone rings the doorbell, and create powerful automations connecting your video intercom with lights, cameras, and other smart devices.

Uses the same secure authentication as the official BTicino Door Entry app, keeping your home security intact.
