This app requires a BTicino "EasyKit" system using the "Door Entry EASYKIT" mobile app.

Seamlessly control your gates, locks, and outdoor video intercom cameras through Homey. Automatically unlock gates when you arrive home, watch live video feeds from your outdoor cameras, receive instant notifications when someone rings the doorbell or when a call is missed, and capture snapshots in your Flows to send with push notifications.

Uses the same secure OAuth2 and mTLS authentication as the official BTicino Door Entry app, keeping your home security intact.
